01 · The concept

Porto and art,
under one roof.

In Porto Gallery Guesthouse was created around a simple idea: Porto and the arts are inseparable. The property brings that idea inside a restored traditional building, combining original works, temporary exhibitions and contemporary interiors.

03 · A guesthouse that behaves like a gallery

Art is not
decoration here.

The property’s strongest distinction is already embedded in its concept: artworks from private collections and temporary exhibitions sit throughout the guesthouse, giving each stay a creative layer that conventional accommodation cannot replicate.

The direct website should make the art concept immediate, visual and memorable from the first screen.
